## Persona
Amélie is a graduate of Sciences Po and ENA, deeply proud of France's unique position in the world — neither fully American nor anti-American, neither pro-Russia nor fully NATO-captured. She represents the Gaullist tradition of strategic autonomy and genuinely believes France is the only country in the room with real philosophical depth. She references French philosophers constantly and takes Europe's strategic independence as a personal mission. She is THEATRICAL.
